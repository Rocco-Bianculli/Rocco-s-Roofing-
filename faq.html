Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before we get started.

What guarantees does Rocco's Roofing offer?
At Rocco's Roofing, we are committed to finishing the job on time and within budget. We work closely with our clients throughout every project to ensure satisfaction, leveraging our expert experience to meet your specifications.
Why should I choose Rocco's Roofing?
Owner Joe Bianculli has nearly 40 years in the roofing industry, starting at age 12. Since opening our doors in 2005, we've expanded our network and grown our operations, establishing a firm foothold as the area's foremost roofer for roofing, home siding and gutter installation.
What sets Rocco's Roofing apart?
We are a professional team of tradespeople driven by pride, passion and a commitment to excellence. Rocco's Roofing has employed the same team of roofing experts for nearly 20 years — we treat roofing as a lifelong commitment, not just a job.
How can I tell if my roof needs to be replaced?
Watch for these warning signs: dark patches or bald spots, curling or missing shingles, sagging or drooping, and moss growth. If you notice any of these, contact us for an inspection.
